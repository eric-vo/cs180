Part B: Feature Matching for Auto-Stitching

For Part B, I implemented the feature-matching pipeline described in “Multi-Image Matching using Multi-Scale Oriented Patches” by Brown et al., using the project’s simplified single-scale, axis-aligned descriptors.

B.1: Harris Corners and ANMS
I converted each image to grayscale and computed the Harris response from smoothed image gradients. I retained local maxima as candidate interest points. The candidate Harris corners are shown in the *_corners figures. These contain redundant detections in textured regions.

I then implemented Adaptive Non-Maximal Suppression. For each candidate, I found the radius of the nearest point with substantially stronger Harris response and selected points with the largest radii. This preserves strong corners while distributing features spatially. The selected points are shown in the *_corners-anms figures.

B.2: Feature Descriptor Extraction
For every selected feature, I sampled a 40x40 window from the blurred grayscale image and downsampled it to an 8x8 axis-aligned descriptor. Sampling from the larger window makes the descriptor less sensitive to noise and small alignment changes. I bias and gain normalized each descriptor by subtracting its mean and dividing by its standard deviation. The *_feature-corners and *_features figures show the locations and representative extracted patches.

B.3: Feature Matching
I compared descriptors with Euclidean distance. For every descriptor in the first image, I found its two nearest neighbors in the second image and accepted the match when the ratio between the nearest and second-nearest distances was sufficiently small. This Lowe-style ratio test removes ambiguous matches. The *_matched figures show the resulting correspondences.

B.4: RANSAC and Automatic Mosaics
To reject remaining outliers, I implemented four-point RANSAC. Each iteration samples four correspondences, computes a homography, and counts inliers using a reprojection-error threshold. I selected the model with the most inliers, recomputed it using all inliers, and passed it to the Part A warping and blending code.

The *_mosaic-manual and *_mosaic-auto figures compare hand-selected and fully automatic stitching for the cafe, apartment, and bedroom scenes. The automatic pipeline successfully recovers the dominant projective alignment for all three mosaics without manually labeling points.
